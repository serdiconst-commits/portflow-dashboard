PortFlow Driver Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 2, 2026

PortFlow Driver is used by drivers to view assigned loads, update load statuses, and upload delivery paperwork for their carrier or dispatch company.

Information We Collect

PortFlow Driver may collect or process:

  • Account information, such as driver name, email address, role, and company assignment.
  • Load information, such as pickup, delivery, container, reference, status, and paperwork details.
  • Documents uploaded by the driver, such as PODs, EIRs, bills of lading, images, and PDF files.
  • Camera or photo library content when a driver chooses to upload paperwork from the device.
  • Basic technical information needed to operate and secure the service.

Camera Permission

The app requests camera access only so drivers can capture and upload load paperwork. Camera access is used only when the driver chooses to take or upload a document.

How Information Is Used

Information is used to:

  • Show assigned loads to drivers.
  • Record load status updates.
  • Store and share delivery paperwork with authorized dispatch or carrier users.
  • Operate, secure, maintain, and improve PortFlow Driver.

Sharing

Driver and load information is shared with authorized users in the same carrier or dispatch company account. We do not sell personal information.

Data Storage and Security

PortFlow Driver uses secure account access and stores operational data on PortFlow's backend systems. No method of electronic storage or transmission is perfectly secure, but reasonable safeguards are used to protect account, load, and document data.

Data Requests

Drivers may contact their carrier or dispatch company to request correction or removal of account, load, or document information.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact PortFlow LLC or the carrier or dispatch company that provided your PortFlow Driver account.
